02 / Overview & methodWorkflow memory and response timing

Observe. Remember.
Respond in time.

SurgStream reads a causal surgical video stream, retains the procedural context and learns to respond within clinically relevant windows.

WAMWhat to remember

Workflow-Anchored Memory

Timestamped instrument–action–target observations are folded into compact phase summaries, preserving a queryable history of the procedure.

Observe records→Phase Summary
DTLWhen to respond

Deadline-aware Transition Learning

Response times are sampled inside evidence-to-deadline windows, teaching the model to reply as early as the evidence allows and before the deadline.

Evidence→Response→Deadline
